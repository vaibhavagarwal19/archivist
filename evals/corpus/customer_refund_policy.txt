Northwind Logistics - Customer Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund for a missed delivery window within 14 days of the scheduled delivery date. Refunds up to 200 USD can be approved by a support agent. Refunds between 200 and 1,000 USD require a support team lead. Refunds above 1,000 USD require the Head of Customer Support.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Damaged-goods claims require photo evidence uploaded within 48 hours of delivery. Enterprise contracts follow their own service credit terms instead of this policy.
