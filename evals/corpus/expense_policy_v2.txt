Northwind Logistics - Expense Policy, Version 2 (effective 1 January 2025)

This version replaces Version 1. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Any single expense above 250 USD requires manager pre-approval, and expenses above 2,000 USD require pre-approval from the department head.

Receipts are required for every expense over 25 USD. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense through the Finance portal. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Home-office equipment is handled by the stipend policy, not through expense claims.
