Northwind Logistics - Health Benefits Enrollment

Open enrollment runs from 1 to 21 November each year. New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll. Qualifying life events such as marriage or the birth of a child allow changes within 30 days of the event.

Employees choose between the Standard plan, where the company pays 85 percent of the premium, and the Premium plan, where the company pays 70 percent. Dependents are covered at the same percentage. Dental and vision are optional add-ons paid fully by the employee. Plan changes outside these windows are not permitted.
