Northwind Logistics - Company Holiday Calendar 2025

There is a company-wide shutdown from 24 December through 1 January. Additional office-specific holidays apply. The United States observes Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, and Thanksgiving with the day after. India observes Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, and two days for Diwali. The United Kingdom observes all England bank holidays.

Employees who work on a company holiday in an on-call capacity receive a day off in lieu within 60 days.
