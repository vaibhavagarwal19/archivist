Northwind Logistics - Leave Policy (India)

Full-time employees in India receive 24 days of paid leave per year, credited on 1 January. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward, and any days above that are encashed at the basic daily rate in March. Leave longer than five days must be requested at least 15 working days in advance through the HR portal. Shorter leave needs 3 working days notice. Managers have 2 working days to respond to a request.

Sick leave is 12 days per year, and a medical certificate is required after 2 consecutive days. Employees also get 3 optional festival holidays chosen from the published regional list.
