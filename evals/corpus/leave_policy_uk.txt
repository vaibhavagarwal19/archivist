Northwind Logistics - Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

Full-time employees in the United Kingdom receive 25 days of annual leave in addition to public bank holidays. A maximum of 3 unused days can be carried over into the next leave year. Absences longer than one week must be requested at least 4 weeks in advance. Shorter absences need 5 working days notice. Line managers should respond within 5 working days.

Sickness is covered by statutory sick pay after 3 waiting days. Employees may self-certify for up to 7 calendar days, and a fit note from a doctor is required after that.
