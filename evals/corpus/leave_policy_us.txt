Northwind Logistics - Leave Policy (United States)

Full-time employees in the United States accrue 18 days of paid time off per year, accruing monthly. Unused days up to a maximum of 5 may be carried into the next calendar year. Employees must request time off in the HR portal at least 10 business days in advance for absences longer than three days. Requests of three days or fewer need 48 hours notice. Managers must approve or decline a request within 3 business days.

Sick leave is separate from paid time off. Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year, with no advance notice required. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days of sick leave.
