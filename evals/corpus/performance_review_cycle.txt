Northwind Logistics - Performance Review Cycle

Reviews run twice a year, with a mid-year review in June and an annual review in December. Employees complete a self-assessment two weeks before the review meeting, and peer feedback is collected from three to five colleagues. Ratings use a five-point scale from Needs Improvement to Exceptional.

Calibration sessions across managers happen the week after reviews close. Promotion cases are submitted only in the December cycle. Compensation changes take effect on 1 February.
