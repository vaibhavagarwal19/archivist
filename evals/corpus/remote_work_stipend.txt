Northwind Logistics - Remote Work and Home Office Stipend

Employees approved for remote work receive a one-time home-office stipend of 600 USD within their first 90 days, plus a monthly internet allowance of 40 USD. Equipment bought with the stipend must be returned or repaid on a pro-rated basis if the employee leaves within 12 months.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Remote employees must visit the nearest office at least once per quarter for team planning.
