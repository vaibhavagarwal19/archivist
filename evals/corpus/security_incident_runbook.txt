Northwind Logistics - Security Incident Runbook

Suspected data exposure, credential compromise, or malware must be reported to security@northwind.example immediately, not through the normal ticket queue. The Security Operations on-call acknowledges within 10 minutes. Affected accounts are disabled first and investigated afterwards.

The Chief Information Security Officer decides whether regulators or customers must be notified, and legal notification clocks start when the CISO confirms a breach. Evidence such as logs and disk images is preserved for 180 days. Engineers must never delete or rotate logs on an affected system before Security Operations approves.
