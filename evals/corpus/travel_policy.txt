Northwind Logistics - Business Travel Policy

Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. All other flights are economy. Book at least 14 days ahead where possible. Hotels are capped at 220 USD per night in tier-one cities and 150 USD elsewhere.

Rental cars are permitted only when public transport is unavailable, and must be the compact class. International travel requires approval from the department head and a travel safety briefing from Security. Travel insurance is purchased centrally and employees should not buy their own.
